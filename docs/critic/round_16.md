## What works

- **Game distance.** It reads as a small work rover: six wheels, an open cab, a solar roof, a loaded
  bed.
- **Scale.** Right next to the suited colonists: boarding, seated with the pack clear, reaching the
  tailgate.
- **Suspension and steering.** The coil-overs show above the fenders. Travel is +0.12 / −0.10 m, and
  both axles steer.
- **Night.** Real headlight beams, tail lights, beacon.
- **Family cues shared with the ships.** Faceted panels, the code RV-01, hazard-stripe bumpers.

## Fixes (ART-B), most important first

1. **Colour.** Six large saturated yellow fenders dominate, and yellow is not a colony accent. Make the
   fenders graphite or off-white, with a thin accent band on the lip, in the colony orange or a family
   accent.
2. **Toy read.** Thick fenders float wide of the wheels, and the body sits high and boxy.
   - Tuck the fenders 6–8 cm above the tyre, with a 3 cm edge.
   - Add a skid plate.
   - Drop the body 10 cm.
3. **Tyres.** 16–20 chunky lugs on the silhouette, darker rubber, and a recessed rim with bolts.
4. **Night.** The bed lamp blows out to a white blob. Halve it and aim it down. Add amber corner markers.
5. **Solar roof.** A frame edge and a slight tilt.

## Steering the medium rover and the hopper

- **Medium rover:**
  - a closed pressurised cabin with portholes, and a hatch that matches the airlock door kit;
  - 6–7 m long, with 8 wheels;
  - the same wheel and suspension parts at 1.3×;
  - an off-white hull with the colony accent band, and a roof rack for Outpost Kits.
- **Hopper:**
  - lander-derived, with legs and thrust bells like the ships' hover thrusters, not wheels;
  - a cockpit bubble and visible tanks;
  - the same code decals and hazard stripes.
- **All vehicles:**
  - at most one accent colour each;
  - graphite running gear and off-white bodies;
  - one night outline pattern: amber corners, red rear, white front.
