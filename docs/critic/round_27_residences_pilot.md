## Fixes (ART-HAB), most important first

1. **Empty drum ring.** The tube stands in a round drum, so inside there is a rectangular block in a
   round hall with a band of empty tile (about 40% of the floor). Fit the pad to the tube, or use the
   ring for private yards and gardens per unit (patio, table, planters, a play corner). Target: no
   empty band wider than 2.5 m.
2. **Executive is larger, not richer.**
   - Wood floors in every room, rugs, wall art, a bookcase.
   - A bed with a headboard, pendant lamps, a plant per room, warmer light.
   - A real office: a desk with two screens and a better chair.
3. **Night.** The rooms are dim and grey. Add warm lamp pools at beds, tables and sofas, and a light
   strip along the street.
4. **Vault exterior.** A plain facetted shell. Add seam lines, a darker base band and visible porch
   lamps. The Executive ridge skylight must read at 250 m.
5. **Family vs Executive at 250 m.** A larger, lit terrace (pergola, loungers) and a second accent on
   the Executive vault.
6. **Children's rooms.** A patterned rug, a bright toy box, a small desk.
7. **ART-HAB / RENDER: evidence for the final.** 250 m by night, the game cutaway in a real colony,
   and the follow view inside a family unit and an executive unit.
