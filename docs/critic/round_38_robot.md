## Tone check (§0): PASS

- **Clearly a machine.** Chrome shell, ball joints, a ribbed waist spring, a visor with a light-bar
  for a face, a chest lamp. No face, no hair, no skin.
- **No human sexual anatomy.** A flat chest plate, and a smooth pelvis with no detail.
- **Poses.**
  - The dances are arm waves, a pirouette and a jump.
  - The pole routine is a walk round, a climb, a hanging tuck, a spin and a flourish.

  They read as gymnastic and performance. There are no hip thrusts, floor work or body rolls, and no
  framing on the pelvis.

**Watch items.**
1. **Pelvis.** From behind it reads as two rounded cheeks. Make it a mechanical hip housing (flat
   sides, a panel line, a joint cap).
2. **Pole frame 20.** A hand-on-hip lean. It is fine; keep it short.
3. **RENDER framing.** Never frame the pelvis in close-up. Shoot the club from mid-distance or wider.

The adults-only door (bouncer and sign) is still owed by ART-B in a render.

2. **ART-NPC: legs.** A knee bounce on every beat, with a 4–6 cm hip drop.
3. **ART-NPC: pelvis.** The mechanical hip housing (watch item 1).
4. **ART-NPC: silhouette.** Thighs 20% thicker and knee guard plates, so it reads as a sturdy robot,
   not a mannequin.
5. **RENDER / ART-B: in game.** The robots on the podiums with the light show, and the bouncer and
   ADULTS ONLY sign at the door, in one frame.
