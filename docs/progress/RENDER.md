### What landed

| part | file | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| Model loader | `presentation/models.gd` | `<id>_<s\|m\|l\|xl>.glb` → `<id>.glb` (scaled by record radius / M radius) → primitive stand-in (rooms, exteriors, Meridian, supply pod, crops, colonist, crate). Each model becomes a template: parts grouped by top-level name (`Base`, `Roof`, `Interior`, `L2..L5`, `Rotor`, `Lights`, `Scaffold`, `EngineGlow`, `Damage1..3`, `Hull`, `Stage1..3`, `Body`, `ArmL/R`, `LegL/R`) plus `Anchor_*` empties. Meshes of one group are merged, one surface per material. Materials are deduplicated into a shared library; surfaces with a COLOR_0 array get `vertex_color_use_as_albedo` (baked AO). Emissive materials (`Window`, `Light`, `Neon`, `Glow`, `Plasma`, level bands) dim by day and brighten at night (`Models.set_night`). `SuitAccent` / `Cargo` are recoloured per instance. |
| Instancing | `presentation/fx_instancer.gd` | Every standing structure, colonist, crop, crate, rock and pod is drawn through ONE MultiMesh per model part. A part hidden on every copy is not drawn. Per copy: transform, hidden groups, extra transform at the group pivot (roof lift, rotor spin, limb swing), custom colour. |
| Structures | `world_view.gd` | Level parts `L2..L5` shown by `b.level`; roof cutaway (nearby roofs open when zoomed in, selected always; glass roofs keep the interior visible); rotors spin with `env.wind`; `Lights` switch on one by one at dusk; crops per tray from `crop_<crop>.glb` (fallback `crop.glb`) with stage by growth; supply pods for piles with `pod == true`; cables as instanced segments. Blueprints = cyan hologram; construction = solid below a glowing build line, hologram above, sparks at the line, dust at the base; demolition = red hologram over the model; broken = darkened + dark smoke + sparks. |
| Colonists | `world_view.gd` | `colonist_suit` outside, `colonist_indoor` inside (fallback `colonist`), role colour on `SuitAccent`, walk swing, work arm motion, sleeping and fallen poses, carried crate tinted by item. |
| Meridian | `presentation/fx_ship.gd` | `Damage1..3` by `ship.stage`, `Scaffold` in stages 1..3, `Lights` from stage 3, `EngineGlow` from stage 4. Test flight from `ship.flight_t` (0..1): rumble, lift 20 m, level out, hover, land with a dust ring and a camera shake; engine plumes from `Anchor_Engine_L/R`. Supply runs (`away`): flies off and returns. Level pose after the flight (per ART-B's pivot notes). |
| Placement API | `presentation/fx_ghost.gd` | `set_ghost(def, size, pos, rot, valid)`: hologram of the real model (scan lines, fresnel, grid; green/red), footprint ring, airlock door strip, dashed suit-range rings round every airlock with air. `clear_ghost()`. `set_link_preview(p0, p1, kind, valid)` for corridors (hologram tube + flowing strip) and cables. |
| Terrain | `presentation/fx_terrain.gd`, `shaders/terrain.gdshader`, `assets/textures/terrain/*.png` | The sim heightfield plus a far ring of hills and mesas to 700 m (one mesh, non-uniform grid). Four height-blended layers (sand with wind ripples, gravel, rock, dark ore field) from tileable 512 px textures generated by `tools/render_textures.py`, detail normals, macro variation, anti-tiling, rock on slopes and shelves. Splat map: ore fields round deposits, rock debris and wind streaks behind rocks, 34 craters with bowls and rims (bent normals). Ground map: footpaths (colonists outside wear the ground where they walk; unused paths fade over about two days; a loaded colony gets its airlock-to-structure routes pre-worn), contact shadow under structures (no SSAO in WebGL), churned ground round construction sites, the walk overlay. Pebble scatter (up to 4 500 twenty-triangle pebbles and 320 `pebbles` patches, 4 MultiMeshes, hidden under structures). |
| Sky and light | `presentation/fx_sky.gd`, `shaders/sky.gdshader` | Sun path over the day, palette keyed on sun elevation: butterscotch day, golden hour, warm sunset band, deep blue dusk, night. Sun disc and glow, dust haze on the horizon, a large pale ringed planet lit by the sun (phase), stars and a galaxy band at night. Key light = sun by day, planet-shine by night. Ambient, fog, glow and exposure follow the palette. Up to 8 real OmniLights (no shadows) placed at the lamps nearest the camera at night; halos and blinking beacons elsewhere. |
| Post | `presentation/fx_post.gd`, `shaders/post_grade.gdshader` | CanvasLayer at layer -5 (the HUD is layer 10): split toning (cool shadows, warm highlights, cooler at night), S-curve contrast, saturation, vignette, light grain, storm tint, flash for `focus_event`. |
| Effects | `presentation/fx_particles.gd`, `shaders/particles_*.gdshader`, `shaders/glow_sprite.gdshader` | GPU particles on MultiMeshes (motion in the vertex shader, no per-frame script work): smoke and steam from working machines (or `Anchor_Smoke`), water spray at extractors, fusion pulse, construction sparks and dust, level-up sparks, dust puffs when pods land, ship plume and dust ring, broken-structure smoke. Dust motes drift near the camera; a dust storm (`state.events.storm`) adds streaking dust, orange fog and a flat sky. |
| Status badges | `presentation/fx_icons.gd`, `shaders/status_icon.gdshader` | One draw call for all badges: bolt (no power), drop (no water), bubbles (no air), wrench (broken), box (output full), empty deposit, hourglass (materials), helmet (too far / out of reach), power button (off), progress ring (construction), cross (removing). Text only for the blocks that need words. |
| Overlays | `presentation/fx_overlay.gd`, `shaders/ground_decal.gdshader` | Power / water / air: one colour per network, flowing lines on the links, rings on the members, red dashed ring = not joined, one label per network with its numbers. Walk: crisp 1 m cells drawn by the terrain shader (open / blocked). All marks drape on the terrain in the vertex shader. |
| Selection | `world_view.gd`, `shaders/outline.gdshader` | Pulsing ground ring plus an inverted-hull outline of constant pixel width on the selected structure. |
| Camera | `presentation/camera_rig.gd` | Damped pan, orbit and zoom (9..200 m); pitch capped close up so the camera looks across the base; fly-in on a new colony (skip with any key or click); `photo_orbit()` for the title screen; `shake()`. |
| Quality | `set_quality(0..3)` | 0: no shadows, no pebbles, no grade, 75 % render scale; 1: ortho shadows 2k, 35 % pebbles; 2 (default): ortho shadows 4k, 70 % pebbles; 3: 2-split shadows, all pebbles, 4x MSAA. Particle counts scale too. |

### Compatibility renderer (Godot 4.4.1, WebGL 2): tested, not guessed

Probe build (removed afterwards), screenshots in the headless GPU browser:

| feature | result | used |
|---|---|---|
| Glow with HDR (emission > 1 blooms) | works | yes |
| Tonemap (Filmic), exposure | works | yes |
| Environment adjustments | works | no (the post shader does the grade) |
| `instance uniform` | works with `material_override` only; NOT with per-surface override materials | not used (construction sites get their own materials) |
| MultiMesh `INSTANCE_CUSTOM`, instance colours | works; custom data WITHOUT instance colours makes `COLOR` zero | instancing (tinted parts carry white colours), particles, badges |
| `next_pass` on per-surface override materials | does NOT draw | hologram as a sibling mesh |
| `hint_screen_texture` in a CanvasLayer shader | works, UI on top unaffected | post grade |
| `hint_depth_texture` in a spatial shader | does NOT work (constant) | no soft particles; particles fade by age instead |
| 3D render scale (bilinear) | works | quality 0/1 |
| `#include` of `.gdshaderinc` | works | particles |
| Depth fog, sun scatter | works | yes |
| Directional shadows ortho / PSSM | works | yes |
| SSAO, SSR, SDFGI, volumetric fog, decals | not in 4.4 Compatibility | replaced by: vertex AO (models), contact-shadow map (terrain), sky reflections, horizon haze in the sky shader + depth fog, terrain-draped decal meshes |

Trap found: fog is added to additive sprites (a lamp halo became a pale square). Every additive,
decal and badge shader has `fog_disabled`.

## 2026-09-24 — milestone 2: verified in the web build, requests answered

### Fixed after screenshots

| problem seen | cause (tested) | fix |
|---|---|---|
| Blueprints drew nothing, then drew solid | In the 4.4.1 Compatibility renderer `next_pass` does not draw on per-surface override materials, and `instance uniform` values do not reach them | each construction site gets its own material copies with plain uniforms; the hologram is a sibling mesh |
| Colonists and crates almost black | A MultiMesh with custom data but no instance colours reads `COLOR` as zero, which blackens vertex-coloured (AO) albedo | tinted batches also carry white instance colours |
| Pale squares at airlocks | fog is added to additive sprites | `fog_disabled` on additive, badge and decal shaders |
| "ORE DEPOSIT" text over the menu | world labels | dashed amber ring on the ground; the word "ORE" only close up; words and badges hide in photo orbit, with a time override, or with `set_labels_visible(false)` |
| Night too blue and dark | | moonlight 0.42 → 0.56, less saturated; night ambient lifted |
| Paths invisible | wear too low | stronger stamping and darkening |
| 4 500 pebbles cost ~700k triangles | rock models used as pebbles | 20-triangle faceted pebble mesh |

### Open issues

   single cost is the HUD (§5 of `RENDER-to-UI.md`); the 3D view alone holds 60+.
